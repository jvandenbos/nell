`2026-10-24-astra-ui-review.md` (U). Fixed between `bbcb708` and `7877475`;
the suite went from 24 to 44 tests. "Live" means the behaviour was driven in
the real QML offscreen with key and mouse events; "static" means read only.

## Noticed on the way, not touched

- Qt only loads an EPUB stylesheet link that has `type="text/css"`; books without it get no CSS (EPUB fidelity).
- `nell info` and `nell notes` still unpack EPUBs into the cache — a cache write from a read-only command.
- `docs/file-formats.md` says the cache can be deleted freely, but covers are only made on add and never regenerated.
- Nothing watches the files: another process's change shows in an open window only after that window's next save.
- QSettings now syncs on every change, including each reading-speed update.
- Highlight colours are still duplicated in `reader.cpp` (~114) and `Tokens.qml`.
- New strings are untranslated in da/de/es-MX/fr/nl.
- "Locate File…" reuses the add-books picker, so the chooser's title says it is adding books.
- The 6 s Undo calls `reader.undo()`, so an action taken inside those 6 s is what gets undone.
- An offscreen-only grey circle at top-left after keyboard or edge-hover steps; probably a Material ripple artefact of the software renderer, unconfirmed.
- The QML driving harness (`shot.cpp`, steps files, screenshots) lives in the session scratchpad only; worth bringing into `tests/` if D10 is to be finished.
